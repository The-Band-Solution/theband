# v0.10.1 — hotfix: the account linked to a person goes through the verdict

**Hotfix**, and not a release from `development`. It is born from `main` (v0.10.0) and carries **one**
fix. It goes back to `main` and, through a `back-merge/v0.10.1` branch, to `development`, always by
merge commit (`AGENTS.md` §12).

**Proposed on 2026-09-28**, under the rule that security comes first (`AGENTS.md` §14.0): active
exposure of personal data in production goes into the **first** possible release. **The moment of
delivery belongs to the maintainer**: every merge into `main` is a deploy.

## What was live

The person's page has a section, *Which account is this person*, that says which platform account
that observed person is. It **had no guard**, and showed to any authenticated account of the tenant:

- the **e-mail** of the account linked to the person, and the date of the link;
- how many accounts the tenant has, and how many have a link.

The API repeated it in `GET /api/v1/people/:id`, in the `account` block: the id of the linked account
and the same coverage.

**There is no data from another tenant.** Within the tenant, any account read the platform e-mail of
any person who had an account, even those the verdict refused it.

**How it was found**: the security assessment of feature 069 (finding E1-3) pointed at the API's
`account` block (#991). While preparing the fix, the screen turned out to be worse. Measured on
2026-09-28 with a `member` account without a link: `{:nao, :sem_elo_declarado}`, and the e-mail of the
linked account on the screen.

## The fix

| Door | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| screen `/people/:id` | the section and the e-mail drawn for everyone, and the list of accounts loaded for everyone | the list only loads with the verdict, and the section only appears with it |
| API `GET /api/v1/people/:id` | `account` always | `null` when `access.can_see_work` is `false`, like `work` |

**Decision of the maintainer, 2026-09-28**: whoever sees the person's work sees their account;
whoever does not, does not. The form that changes the link remains admin-only, and the admin always
has reach.

> (original: "quem vê o trabalho da pessoa vê a conta dela; quem não vê, não vê.")

061's contract and the OpenAPI schema were amended: `account` is `nullable`, with the reason.

Checked alongside: the other places that show an account's e-mail (`/accounts`, `/access-scopes`,
`/api-tokens`) are in the `live_session :admin`, with `require_admin`. The person's page was the only
one.

## Why PATCH

| Degree | Carries it? | Why |
|---|---|---|
| MAJOR | no | the response for whoever **has** reach does not change. What changes for whoever does not is the leak, as with #989 in v0.10.0 |
| MINOR | no | nothing new that someone can now do |
| **PATCH** | **yes** | it makes the screen and the route meet 023's FR-012h: the refusal comes before the load |

## The evidence

- `test/the_band_web/conta_dentro_do_veredito_test.exs`: five tests, both doors, with a person
  linked to an account with a unique e-mail. The refused account does not see the e-mail, the
  coverage or the section, and receives `account: null`; the admin sees the e-mail and the id.
- **Injected defect**: `main`'s screen in place of the fixed one fails 1 of 5 (*"a tela mostrou o
  e-mail da conta a quem o veredito recusa"*); `main`'s API fails 1 of 5.
- An existing test, in `elo_da_conta_na_tela_test.exs`, asserted that the account without reach saw
  the sentence *"Only an administrator can change this link"*, which sits inside the section. It was
  replaced by "the section does not appear". What it guards remains: the event sent directly is
  refused on the server.
- `mix gates`: the exit code is on the PR.

## What the fix does not undo: who may have seen it

**An e-mail that has already been seen does not come back.** The trail is the same as #989's in
v0.10.0:

1. **on the screen**, every refused view of a person's page generated the
   `acesso: painel recusado` event in the log, with `user_id`, `tenant_id` and `alvo_person_id`. If
   the person had a linked account, that account saw the e-mail;
2. **in the API**, `api_access_reads` with `route = '/api/v1/people/:id'`: the tokens that read each
   person. The API exposed the account's id, and not the e-mail.

The retention of the production log was not measured. The searches run in production, and are an
act of whoever operates.

## What shipped

The whole platform, for the first time outside the repository: multi-stage image,
CD on push to `main`, the Dokploy runbook, and the entrypoint that checks the variables
before starting ([#632](https://github.com/The-Band-Solution/theband/pull/632)),
plus the public front door speaking through the metaphor
([#637](https://github.com/The-Band-Solution/theband/pull/637)).

## What production revealed, and the suite would not

Recorded in [medicao-do-primeiro-release.md](../../specs/050-em-producao/medicao-do-primeiro-release.md)
and in [pendencias.md](../../specs/050-em-producao/pendencias.md):

- **the LiveView socket refused with 403 while HTTP answered 200** —
  `_mount_attempts => "79"` in the log, and a loading bar that never finished
  (it became lesson L85, and is the origin of feature 054);
- **the panel saying `Done` with the container dying in a loop** (L84);
- **nobody could get in** — there was no account, and `seeds.exs` raises in
  production on purpose. That is what produced 052 and the following release.

## What proved to be right

The entrypoint brought the container down when it could not resolve the database, instead of serving zero
on every screen; the migration ran before the endpoint started answering; and the image carries
no secret at all — `docker history` and `Config.Env` with **0** occurrences.

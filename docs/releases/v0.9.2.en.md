# v0.9.2 — hotfix: the API roster stops returning e-mail, and stops breaking on whoever left

**Hotfix**, and not a release from `development`. It is born from `main` (v0.9.1) and carries **one** fix,
with one line of test dependency. It goes back to `main` and to `development` by merge commit
(`AGENTS.md` §12).

**Proposed on 2026-09-25**, under the rule that security comes first (`AGENTS.md` §14.0):
active exposure in production goes into the **first** possible release. **The moment of delivery belongs to the
maintainer**: every merge into `main` is a deploy.

## What was live, since v0.9.0

`GET /api/v1/teams/:id/members`, the roster route of the public API:

| # | Defect | Severity | Consequence |
|---|---|---|---|
| **N1** | a team membership marked as a mistake came out with `mistake.por`, the **e-mail** of whoever marked it | high | any token with reach over the team read the e-mail of whoever administers. The route's own OpenAPI schema says e-mail does not come out |
| **N2** | an ended team membership came out with `ended_at` equal to the tuple `{:declarado, <e-mail>, …}`, which Jason does not serialize | medium | the route answered **`500`** for every team with someone who left, and the **e-mail** of whoever declared the departure went to the **error log** |

Found on 2026-09-25 by the security review of the MCP server implementation
(`specs/062-servidor-mcp/seguranca-revisao-da-implementacao.md`), which saw the same defect in the API.
**No test caught it**: the fixture only had an observed, in-force team membership.

## The fix

- `ended_at` becomes the **date**, which is what the schema already declared;
- `end_origin`, **new field**: `declared`, `no_longer_observed` or `declared_without_author`. The
  date of a departure via collection is when the platform stopped seeing the person, and not when they left;
- `mistake` keeps `reason` and `at`, **without the author**;
- `lazy_html` 0.1.12 → 0.1.13, a test-only dependency, with `EEF-CVE-2026-92106` (LOW), which
  made `mix hex.audit` exit 1.

## Why PATCH

| Degree | Carries? | Why |
|---|---|---|
| MAJOR | no | nothing the contract promised stops holding |
| MINOR | no | there is nothing new that users can now do |
| **PATCH** | **yes** | it makes the route fulfill what the schema already said: e-mail does not come out, and `ended_at` is a date. `end_origin` is not new functionality, it is the information the broken tuple carried, now serializable |

**What changes for integrators, and is stated**: `mistake.por` disappears, and it should never have come out.
Whoever read `ended_at` received `500`, and now receives a date.

## What the fix does not undo: who may have read it

**The e-mail that already went out does not come back.** What can be known is **who read it**, and the read record
makes it possible to know everything: the `/members` route (#934) and the record (#936) went to production **together**, in
v0.9.0. Checked on 2026-09-25 with `git merge-base --is-ancestor` on the tags: neither of the two
is in v0.8.0, and both are in v0.9.0. **There was no read of that route in production without a
record.**

The query, to run **on the production database** (an act of whoever operates, through the `deploy-producao` agent
or by the maintainer; no agent in this session accesses production):

```sql
-- Quem leu /members de uma equipe que já tinha um equívoco marcado no momento da leitura.
-- Só essas leituras podem ter levado o e-mail (N1). As do N2 deram 500, e 500 não é gravado.
SELECT r.token_public_id,
       r.target_id              AS team_id,
       count(*)                 AS leituras,
       min(r.occurred_at)       AS primeira,
       max(r.occurred_at)       AS ultima
FROM api_access_reads r
WHERE r.route = '/api/v1/teams/:id/members'
  AND EXISTS (
    SELECT 1
    FROM eo_team_memberships m
    WHERE m.team_id = r.target_id
      AND m.invalidated_at IS NOT NULL
      AND m.invalidated_at <= r.occurred_at
  )
GROUP BY r.token_public_id, r.target_id
ORDER BY ultima DESC;
```

**How to read the result**:

- **no rows**: no token read the e-mail, and N1 has no consequence;
- **with rows**: each `token_public_id` is a credential that received the e-mail of whoever marked the
  mistake. The tokens screen, `/api-tokens`, says whom each one belongs to. If any is not
  recognized, **revoke it with the reason `suspeita de vazamento`** (suspected leak), and notify whoever had their e-mail
  exposed.

**And the log**: the `500`s of N2 wrote the e-mail of whoever declared the departure into the application's error
log. Whoever has access to the production log had access to it. It is the same group that operates, but
it is stated.

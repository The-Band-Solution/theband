Syncs: when the job queue stops

The /syncs screen today shows a run as running whether its work is moving or abandoned. This prototype adds one thing: the screen says when the job queue has not moved, for how long, what that means for the runs listed, and what the person operating can do. Who sees it: administrators and people with an organisation grant, the same as today.

Rule behind every state (contract in docs/producao/saude-da-fila.md, TheBand.Saude.fila/2): the queue is stalled when the last finished job is 15 min old or more, or when no job ever finished and a job has waited 15 min or more. 15 min is three cycles of the 5-minute scheduler.

Decisions and open questions

Approved by the maintainer on 2026-10-01: D1–D6, Q1–Q3 (option A each). P1 still needs an owner. The security review of D3 (queue state shown to organisation-scoped viewers) is in progress.

Decided 2026-10-01 · maintainer

D1. Fact first, verdict second, action third

The observed time (last job finished) carries the observed mark; "stalled" carries the derived mark and states its rule. The notice never says "error", "failure" or "down".

Decided 2026-10-01 · maintainer

D2. The run record is not rewritten

The running badge stays. A second mark, not advancing · queue stalled, sits beside it, with the two statements side by side (055 FR-012).

Decided 2026-10-01 · maintainer

D3. No count of waiting jobs, no worker names, no tenants

The job table is shared by every tenant. The notice shows only times. A count of waiting jobs would tell an organisation-scoped viewer how much other tenants have queued. Same reasoning as /health, which says only queue stalled.

Decided 2026-10-01 · maintainer

D4. The minutes keep counting without a reload

With the queue stalled, no progress event reaches the screen, so a number computed once would freeze. The screen re-checks every minute and shows the check time.

Decided 2026-10-01 · maintainer

D5. "Close stuck sync" keeps today's rule

A run whose work is waiting in the queue is not offered closing; the card says why and points to the restart. A run whose job is stuck in executing keeps today's button and confirmation text.

Decided 2026-10-01 · maintainer

D6. Duration format

Under an hour in minutes (47 min), under a day in hours and minutes (3 h 12 min), then days and hours (4 d 2 h). Always with the absolute UTC time next to it.

Premise · needs an owner

P1. The runbook link must point to a section that exists

docs/producao/runbook.md has no stalled-queue section today. The screen's link and its steps depend on it. Recommendation: write it in the same delivery, before the screen links to it.

Decided 2026-10-01 · maintainer · option A

Q1. Normal state: a quiet line, or nothing?

  • A. One line under the header with the observed time of the last finished job (screen 1).
  • B. Nothing until the queue stalls.

Decided: A. Without it, "no warning" and "the check did not run" look the same, and the line costs one row.

Decided 2026-10-01 · maintainer · option A

Q2. Sync and Reprocess while the queue is stalled

  • A. Disabled, with the reason next to the button (screens 2 and 3).
  • B. Enabled; the notice warns that new work will wait.

Decided: A. Pressing Sync creates another running record that cannot advance, which is the defect this notice exists to expose.

Decided 2026-10-01 · maintainer · option A

Q3. The screen needs more than fila/2 returns

  • A. A new read function next to it, returning last finished time, oldest waiting time and the check time; fila/2 and /health unchanged.
  • B. Change fila/2 to return the case and the timestamps.

Decided: A. {:parada, minutos} does not say which of the two cases applies, and screens 2 and 3 say different things. Changing fila/2 would touch the healthcheck contract for a screen's need.

# Resume — the state lives at the repository root {#retomar--o-estado-vive-na-raiz-do-repositório}

The state document is the **[`RETOMAR.md` at the root](../../RETOMAR.md)**.

## Why not here {#por-que-não-aqui}

There were **two**: this one and the one at the root. On 2026-09-09 one stood at 08/26 and the other at 09/03, and
each described a different product — v0.4.0 and v0.6.0. Two state documents that
diverge are worse than one that is out of date: whoever reads one of them does not know the other exists.

The root won because it is where you land first when opening the repository, and because the content
**does not belong on a published site**: it names unmerged branches, open PRs and
open security findings. None of that is technical reference — it is the session's notebook.

## What remains here, in `docs/sprints/` {#o-que-continua-aqui-em-docssprints}

- **[Lessons learned](licoes-aprendidas.md)** — what holds beyond a single session;
- the **per-sprint** records: backlog, review and acceptance.

The difference is one of nature: the state **changes every session** and goes stale within days; the lesson and the
sprint record are permanent, and that is why these stay on the site and that one does not.

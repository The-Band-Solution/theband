Decisions and open questions

What this prototype settles

Decided 2026-09-29 by the maintainer · D1 · inherits the approved list The field uses the list's component, marks and tooltip text unchanged.

Solid verdete for the field, hatched and outlined blue for the title, dashed for absence — exactly as approved for /work on 2026-09-13, including the two title strings. The component already exists (<.rotulos>); the detail calls it instead of writing badge-ghost by hand. Blue for derived stays, because it is what the approved list uses; changing one screen alone would make the same label read two ways.

Decided 2026-09-29 by the maintainer · D2 · no cut Every label is shown. No three-and-+N.

The cut belongs to the row, where height is shared. The +N in the list is a link to this page with the promise that all labels are here.

Decided 2026-09-29 by the maintainer · D3 · order and place Field labels first, then the title's; the field stays where it is.

Same order as Rotulos.de/2 and the list (FR-012). The row keeps its place between assignees and milestone in “As the source describes it”.

Decided 2026-09-29 by the maintainer · Q1 · written origin — (a) Write the origin beside each group of marks, or rely on the tooltip as the list does?
  • (a) one written line per origin, as shown — observed — set on the label field at the source;
  • (b) marks only, with the title tooltip and no visible text.

Decided: (a), as recommended. The list can lean on a legend and the tooltip because it has no room; this field has room. A tooltip does not reach a keyboard, a phone or a greyscale capture, and SC-003 is measured on the capture.

Decided 2026-09-29 by the maintainer · Q2 · the card's heading — (a) Can a derived label sit under “As the source describes it”?
  • (a) yes, in the same field, with the derived line saying it was read from the title;
  • (b) move the derived line to the Promotion card, where the platform's own readings live;
  • (c) rename the card.

Decided: (a), as recommended. The title is what the source describes; the mark and the written line say the reading is the platform's. Splitting would break the independent test of US1 — “the same labels appear when each item is opened” — in one field.

Decided 2026-09-29 by the maintainer · Q3 · the absence sentence — (a) How much should “no label” say?
  • (a) the sentence shown: none on the label field at the source, and no declared prefix in the title;
  • (b) also name a bracket that did not qualify — [Portal ADM] is not a declared prefix;
  • (c) only no label, as in the list.

Decided: (a), as recommended. It says whose absence each half is, as the design system asks, and needs nothing the platform does not already compute. (b) names the list of rejected brackets on screen, which is a new rule to test; it can come later if people ask why a bracket did not count.

Out of scope: case-insensitive matching of title prefixes (the 47 variants such as [DevOps] that do not derive today) is not part of this screen; it is its own feature, #1023.

No new measure. The field shows labels and their origin, both already in the knowledge base and in Rotulos.de/2. Nothing here needs a new YAML.

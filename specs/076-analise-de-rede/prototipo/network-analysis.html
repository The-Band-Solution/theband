Network Analysis

A new area in the main menu. Its first page is the review network of spec 073; the other pages read the same network, and the delegation network, the way the reference report does: the two graphs, the communities, the hubs, distance and the small-world test, each person's position, and each person's profile. Every number is rounded and said in words, and nothing missing is drawn as zero.

Every state must read without colour.

All names, organisations, dates and counts are example. The people are fictional (“… Example”). The scale follows the reference report: 49 people and 168 review links, 50 people and 169 delegation links. Every measure on a screen is computed by the prototype's generator from one example set of links, with a fixed seed, so the numbers agree with each other.

Network analysis

Read the links between people as a network.

Every page reads one of two links. You choose which on each page; the choice is kept while you move between pages.

review
a person reviewed a change request someone else opened. The arrow goes from the reviewer to the author.
delegation
a person opened an issue and someone else is its assignee. The arrow goes from the author of the issue to the assignee.
  • Review network

    Is code review concentrated in a few people?

    spec 073 · the first page · already approved

  • Graph

    Who is linked to whom, and who sits between groups?

    size = people linked · colour = betweenness · width = links

  • Communities

    Which groups work mostly among themselves?

    modularity · 6 communities · members · the most linked in each

  • Hubs

    Who is central, and in which sense?

    degree · betweenness · closeness · eigenvector

  • Distance and small world

    How many steps separate people, and is the network a small world?

    average distance · diameter · efficiency · clustering · σ

  • Positions and profiles

    Where does each person sit, and who do they work with?

    position in the network · who they assign to and receive from

These pages describe how work flows between people in the observed repositories. They do not assess anyone: who reviews or assigns follows role, team, time off and who was asked.

decisions and open questions
Decided 2026-10-03 · by the maintainerThe two graphs of the reference

Weighted: size = people linked, colour = betweenness, width = count. Communities: colour by community. Both on review or delegation, chosen on the page.

Decided 2026-10-03 · by the maintainerTwo named links, never “collaboration”

review: reviewer → author of the change request. delegation: author of the issue → assignee.

Decided 2026-10-03 · by the maintainerInteractive, laid out on the server

Positions computed once per reading; the page zooms, pans and highlights. On the phone the graph becomes the list.

Decided 2026-10-03 · by the maintainerNames only within reach

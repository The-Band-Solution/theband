Flow

The grain switch changes the axis, never the measure: a month is the same items counted by month. Burn-up and burn-down · by week · 14 Jul → 8 Sep

weekmonthyear

The scope line starts at what was already open when the window opened. Without that baseline the cumulative counts only the items born inside the window, and a team with 834 old items and few new ones would show a distance of zero. What is still open is the hatched region between the two lines — a distance, not a third line (057, FR-027). No committed scope line: the team has not declared one.

scope · open at the start, plus opened sinceclosed, cumulative in the windowstill open = distance

open(t) = 834 + opened(t₀…t) − closed(t₀…t) — the identity the chart draws, and the reason the baseline is on it.

The gap is 841 items still open. At the closing pace observed in this window — 5.0 closed per week — most runs never reach zero inside the 12-week horizon: the pace does not keep up with what is open. A range with its confidence, never a date (asked for on 8 Sep).

Delivery forecast · weekly, whatever grain the charts show · 12-week horizon · 10 000 runs

How long to close the 841 items open today, sampling this team's own weekly closing history (flow.completion.forecast). Renamed from "Monte Carlo forecast" on 8 Sep: the method is not the answer, and the maintainer asked what the card was.

Two hypotheses, stacked, on the same x axis — never overlaid: two translucent shapes on one pair of axes make a third shape that does not exist. Stacked, each with its own y, the horizontal distance between the peaks is the cost of the new work arriving. The shape answers how regularly; the percentile answers by when; neither replaces the other.

why there is no number here

A percentile of a hypothesis whose runs did not finish is null, never a large number: a large number would be read as a date. And when most runs do not finish, the percentiles would describe a minority — so the proportion that did not finish is part of the result, not a footnote (flow.completion.forecast, limitations). Below the floor — 6 periods in the series and 10 closed items — the forecast is not produced at all.

Both hypotheses sample a pace made almost entirely of items opened inside the window, while the 834 older ones did not move. So the card answers "how long at this pace", and not "when the stock goes" — see the open question at the end of the page.

Decisions and open questions

decide before the code
  1. Dashboard and Structure as two tabs of /teams/:id. Decided 7 Sep: tabs; the URL keeps the tab (?tab=structure) so a link lands where it points.
  2. "Mistake" on an observed link. Decided 7 Sep: allowed. The mistake is recorded; the next collection does not re-associate; the screen shows both statements while the source disagrees.
  3. Direct members of a composed team. Decided 7 Sep: yes — the row "direct members" on the dashboard and the chip "direct" in the member list stay.
  4. Charts on the composed team's dashboard — your request of 7 Sep amends 057 FR-011 ("no charts on the composed team"). The squad page keeps its own charts; the team dashboard shows the whole team's flow. Confirm the amendment.
  5. What "committed" means. Decided 7 Sep: opened in the period; "delivered" is done in the period. No iteration needed.
  6. Projects on the dashboard. Decided 7 Sep: only declared links are projects; work outside any declared project raises an alert (who, where, how much) — never a project row.
  7. Month and year grain. Same items, coarser axis. Monte Carlo stays weekly (its samples are weeks); month/year only re-bin burn and committed × delivered.
  8. Thresholds of "Problems now". Decided 7 Sep: 30 days for issues and 7 days for reviews are confirmed and get their YAML with the decision they support; tasks use the 90 days the base already declares.
  9. Windows. Decided 7 Sep: defaults are fixed — 56 days for the measures; 8 weeks · 12 months · all years per grain for the charts — and the person can choose another period; the title always names the window shown.
  10. Departure without a date is refused; "Declare all roles" stays. Decided 7 Sep.
  11. Profile on the dashboard. Decided 7 Sep: up to four skills, floor of 15 described tasks, and a link to the person's full profile.
  12. The composed team. Decided 7 Sep: the organisation declares "Conecta Fapes" and composes the squads into it; the derived team keeps its own rule alongside.
  13. Who sees and who acts. Decided 7 Sep: names, tasks and profiles for administrators, scope holders and the team's own members; structure actions for administrators and for a team-manager role granted "manage team structure".
  14. Work outside a declared project = open issues and change requests in repositories that belong to no board of a declared project, plus boards linked to no project. Decided 7 Sep.
  15. Roles here and at /roles. The prototype creates roles from the team's structure screen — the same command, the same organisation scope. Does /roles stay as the organisation-wide view (grants of visibility live there), or does it fold into this screen?
  16. The mark of the concept. Decided 8 Sep: every work item opens with what it is, in the place of the source's node_id. Six clauses, because two different ignorances are two different statements. Hue stays with origin and state; the concept family is told apart by form — and borrows one hue, clay, for the defect alone.
  17. A fixed 3.5 rem slot for the mark, right-aligned. Decided 8 Sep: fifteen items of one person began on fifteen different left edges. Only the untranslated identifier may outgrow the slot, and it pushes its own line.
  18. Stale moves from clay to amber. Decided 8 Sep, and this one goes the implementation's way. Three reasons: the flags column of this very screen already marks the same fact in amber; the house grammar reads amber as "derived and warning", which is what a threshold crossing is; and with the defect now taking clay, and nearly every item past the threshold, clay would paint the whole list and stop meaning gravity. The text stays stopped · over 90 d — a threshold is part of its measure.
  19. The burn opens at the window's opening balance. Decided 8 Sep: the identity open(t) = open(t₀) + opened − closed is written under the chart, because without the baseline a team with a large old stock shows a distance of zero.
  20. Items past the threshold: one row per squad, no total. Decided 8 Sep: an item of a person in two squads would be counted twice (057, FR-008), so that card refuses the total the way every other measure of a composed team does.
  21. Names of the flow charts. Decided 8 Sep: "Committed × delivered" becomes Promised × Delivered — the maintainer's own word, and "committed" claimed a commitment the platform does not hold — and "Monte Carlo forecast" becomes Delivery forecast: the method is not the answer.

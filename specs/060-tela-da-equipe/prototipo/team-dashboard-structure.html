screen 1 · dashboard
what this screen is The measures, and under each one who it was measured over. A composed team shows one row per squad and never a total — the same person can be in two squads and the same task would be counted twice (057, FR-008). For the manager, in this order: one card per squad (a summary and a door to the squad's own page); problems now; the measures, one row per squad; the team's flow charts; the people — direct and through the squads — with what each is on now and what each has demonstrated; the projects. The flow charts — burn-up/burn-down, committed × delivered, Monte Carlo — by week, month or year. This amends 057 FR-011 ("no charts on the composed team"), by decision of the maintainer on 7 Sep.

Decisions and open questions

decide before the code
  1. Dashboard and Structure as two tabs of /teams/:id. Decided 7 Sep: tabs; the URL keeps the tab (?tab=structure) so a link lands where it points.
  2. "Mistake" on an observed link. Decided 7 Sep: allowed. The mistake is recorded; the next collection does not re-associate; the screen shows both statements while the source disagrees.
  3. Direct members of a composed team. Decided 7 Sep: yes — the row "direct members" on the dashboard and the chip "direct" in the member list stay.
  4. Charts on the composed team's dashboard — your request of 7 Sep amends 057 FR-011 ("no charts on the composed team"). The squad page keeps its own charts; the team dashboard shows the whole team's flow. Confirm the amendment.
  5. What "committed" means. Decided 7 Sep: opened in the period; "delivered" is done in the period. No iteration needed.
  6. Projects on the dashboard. Decided 7 Sep: only declared links are projects; work outside any declared project raises an alert (who, where, how much) — never a project row.
  7. Month and year grain. Same items, coarser axis. Monte Carlo stays weekly (its samples are weeks); month/year only re-bin burn and committed × delivered.
  8. Thresholds of "Problems now". The base declares one today: 90 days open (profile.thresholds.stale_open_work). "30 days" for issues and "7 days" for reviews do not exist yet — either they are declared in a YAML with the decision they support, or those two cards are not built (principle IV).
  9. Roles here and at /roles. The prototype creates roles from the team's structure screen — the same command, the same organisation scope. Does /roles stay as the organisation-wide view (grants of visibility live there), or does it fold into this screen?
